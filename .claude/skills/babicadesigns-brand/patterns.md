# BabicADesigns Brand Patterns

## Pattern: Voice Calibration Check
**When to use**: Before publishing any piece of content — post, email, caption, bio, product description.
**How**:
1. Read the draft aloud.
2. Ask: Does this sound SOULFUL? (emotion, warmth, specificity) → If no, add a human detail or story.
3. Ask: Is this STRATEGIC? (frameworks, concrete steps, actionable) → If no, add the "how," not just the "what."
4. Ask: Is this REAL? (no hype, no jargon, no abstract inspiration) → If no, delete the performative sentence.
5. Ask: Would a trusted older sister say this? → If it sounds like a blog from 2018 or a guru on a webinar, rewrite.
**Trade-offs**: Takes 3 extra minutes per piece. Prevents voice drift that compounds over months.

## Pattern: The Drop Structure
**When to use**: Every Tuesday newsletter.
**How**:
1. Line 1: "Grab your kava. This one's practical. ☕" — never vary this
2. Line 2-3: Name the real problem (not the stated one) in 2 short sentences
3. Next paragraph: Signal the structure without naming it ("Three things. No fluff.")
4. Body: Deliver the practical thing in numbered steps or tight paragraphs
5. Close: One sentence on why this matters. Then: "Until Tuesday — Anita" or similar
**Trade-offs**: The fixed opener is a constraint and a brand asset. Consistency builds recognition; deviation breaks the ritual.

## Pattern: Max-3 Color Composition
**When to use**: Any designed asset — social posts, covers, slides, PDFs, carousels.
**How**:
1. Start: Warm Cream `#F0E6D3` as background
2. Add: Deep Charcoal `#1C1917` for all typography
3. Choose ONE accent: Dusty Rose `#C4A09A` for tagline/emotional emphasis OR Sage Green `#8A9E89` for labels/detail — not both in the same layout
4. Stop. Do not add a fourth color.
5. B mark: Dark Sage Green `#385048` — this counts as the third if used
**Trade-offs**: Feels restrictive. Creates instant visual recognition that compounds over time. The constraint IS the aesthetic.

## Pattern: AI Image Prompt Assembly
**When to use**: Generating any illustration for the brand.
**How**:
1. Open with Master Style Instruction (verbatim — see Ch 5)
2. Specify format: "16:9, 1920×1080" or "9:16, 1080×1920" or "1:1, 1080×1080"
3. Add protagonist description (see Ch 5 for full text block)
4. Specify outfit: A (seated/indoor), B (outdoor/standing), or C (desk/working)
5. Describe the scene from the canonical 10 scenes, or specify a new scene precisely
6. Add: "Warm golden light from upper left."
7. Add extended illustration palette hex codes
8. Close with: "Bold clean linework, flat color fills, soft gradient shading. No text in image."
**Trade-offs**: More text per prompt = more consistent output. Skipping any section risks style drift.

## Pattern: 1-to-6 Repurposing
**When to use**: After every published piece of content.
**How**:
1. Identify the single most quotable/actionable line in the piece
2. Thread: Rewrite as a 5-post Twitter thread (hook → 3-4 key points → CTA/link)
3. Substack note: One-sentence observation from the piece, under 120 chars
4. Carousel: Title slide + 5 slides (one point each) + consistent visual treatment
5. Reels/TikTok hook: The most counterintuitive or surprising insight in 3 seconds
6. Story teaser: One image (illustration) + one provocative question
7. Single image: Best sentence over a scene illustration background
**Trade-offs**: Feels like "more work." Creates 6x presence from 1x effort. The repurposing IS the content strategy.

## Pattern: Balkan Word Placement
**When to use**: Voice flavor — not every post, but regularly enough to be consistent.
**How**:
- **kava**: In place of "coffee" — natural, never explained. Best in Drop opener or casual moments.
- **polako**: When slowing the reader down, anti-hustle moments. "Polako. One thing at a time."
- **ajde**: Transition / gentle nudge. "Ajde, let's get into it."
- **ma daj**: Shared exasperation. "Ma daj — you don't need another template."
- **jebiga**: Rueful acceptance, earned use only. Feels most authentic at paragraph or section end.
- **ništa**: Dismissal of the obvious or unnecessary. "Ništa — skip the intro, here's the thing."
**Trade-offs**: Used sparingly = authentic cultural texture. Used too often = affectation. 1-2 per piece maximum.

## Pattern: Signature Phrase Templates
**When to use**: Opening or transition sentences in any long-form piece.
**How** (use any of these formulations):
- "The thing most people get wrong about [X] is..."
- "Let me show you exactly what I do when [situation]."
- "Here's what changed everything for me: [specific insight]."
- "Grab your kava. This one's practical. ☕" (Drop opener only)
**Trade-offs**: These phrases signal "BabicADesigns" to returning readers. Using too many in one piece becomes formulaic.

## Pattern: Typography Assignment
**When to use**: Any text in a designed layout.
**How**:
1. Hero headline / display text → Cormorant Garamond Semibold or Bold
2. Section headers / subheadings → Playfair Display Regular
3. Body copy / long-form / Substack → EB Garamond Regular
4. Tagline "The Balkanish AI Way" → classical cursive script, Dusty Rose, always
5. Never combine more than 2 typefaces in one layout
6. Never use a typeface in the wrong tier (no Playfair for body, no EB Garamond for hero)
**Trade-offs**: Strict hierarchy creates visual authority. Mixing tiers creates visual noise.

## Pattern: Audience Filter Test
**When to use**: Before creating any content piece, product offer, or campaign.
**How**:
1. Does this speak to the core fear: "AI will make me sound like everyone else"?
2. Does this fulfill the desire: "A system that works AND still sounds like me"?
3. Does this deliver on the need: "A trusted guide, not another guru"?
4. Is this speaking to someone experienced, not a beginner?
5. Is the value deliverable same-day, or at least this week?
If any answer is no — adjust the framing, not the content.
**Trade-offs**: Narrows the potential audience. Creates deep resonance with the exact right person.

**When to use**: Any formal introduction, media kit, email footer, platform bio.
**Standard block**:

Anita Babic
Soulful AI Strategist | Brand Designer
anita@babicadesigns.store | babicadesigns.blog
@BabicADesigns (Instagram, Substack)

**Trade-offs**: Title precision matters. "Soulful AI Strategist | Brand Designer" is the exact phrasing — don't abbreviate or reorder.
