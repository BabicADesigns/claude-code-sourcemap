# BabicADesigns Brand Cheatsheet

## Brand Promise
> "Make AI feel human. Make business feel intentional."

## Voice in 3 Words
**SOULFUL. STRATEGIC. REAL.**

## Voice Archetype
Trusted older sister who figured it out. Smart friend giving advice over kava. Cultural, grounded, quietly confident.

## One-liner
"I help creators build content systems that actually sound like them — with AI, strategy, and a little Balkan spice."

---

## Color Palette

| Color | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Warm Cream | `#F0E6D3` | Background — everything breathes on this |
| Deep Charcoal | `#1C1917` | All typography |
| Dark Sage Green | `#385048` | B mark only |
| Dusty Rose | `#C4A09A` | Tagline + ONE accent per composition |
| Sage Green | `#8A9E89` | Labels + secondary details |

**Rule**: Never more than 3 colors in one composition.

---

## Typography

| Typeface | Weight | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Cormorant Garamond | Semibold/Bold | Hero display — anything eye should linger on |
| Playfair Display | Regular | Subheadings, section headers |
| EB Garamond | Regular | Body, long-form, Substack |
| Cursive script | — | Tagline "The Balkanish AI Way" — Dusty Rose only |

**Rule**: Never mix more than 2 typefaces in one layout.

---

## Content Formats

| Format | Day | Length | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Drop ☕ | Tuesday | 400–600 words | Workshop, practical, same-day use |
| The Gentle Note 🌿 | Alt. Thursday | 600–900 words | Letter, emotional, slow |
| Creator System Series 📐 | Alt. Thursday | 900–1,400 words | Framework, deep-dive, authoritative |

**The Drop always opens**: "Grab your kava. This one's practical. ☕"

## AI Image Prompt — Quick Reference

**Master Style** (always first):
> "clean flat cel-shading digital illustration; bold clean linework, flat color fills with soft gradient shading; NOT watercolor, NOT painterly, NOT photorealistic, NOT Pixar 3D; think high-quality editorial fashion illustration"

**Protagonist**:
> "woman, long wavy light golden blonde hair, natural curvy body with real feminine proportions (NOT model-thin), confident and grounded, blue-green eyes, gold hoop earrings, warm skin tone, late 30s energy, sophisticated but approachable"

**Light**: "Warm golden light from upper left."

**Always close with**: "Bold clean linework, flat color fills, soft gradient shading. No text in image."

**Outfits**:
- A (seated/indoor): white linen blouse + sage green wide-leg trousers
- B (outdoor): white linen blouse + sage green midi skirt + brown sandals
- C (desk): white linen blouse French-tucked + sage green straight-leg trousers

**Formats**: 16:9 (1920×1080) | 9:16 (1080×1920) | 1:1 (1080×1080)

## Balkan Words (use naturally, never explain)
- **kava** — coffee
- **polako** — slowly, easy does it
- **ajde** — let's go / come on
- **ma daj** — oh come on (gentle pushback)
- **jebiga** — what can you do (rueful acceptance)
- **ništa** — nothing / never mind

---

## Anti-patterns — Never Do This

**Voice**: corporate language, guru energy, hype words ("game-changer," "unlock"), abstract advice without method, passive voice, robotic AI phrasing.

**Visual**: warm beige/terracotta backgrounds, neon/gradients/AI glow, crowded layouts, generic stock photos (laptops, women smiling), cold blue/purple lighting, more than 2 accent colors, model-thin protagonist.

**Content**: posting without a hook, mixing format tones (Drop energy in Gentle Note), repurposing by copy-paste without rebuilding for format, skipping Substack notes.

## Audience Profile (One Person)
- Late 30s–40s emotionally intelligent solopreneur
- Tech-overwhelmed, voice-protective
- **Fear**: "AI will make me sound like everyone else"
- **Desire**: "A system that works — and still sounds like me"
- **Need**: "A trusted guide. Not another guru."
