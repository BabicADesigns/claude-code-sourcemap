# BabicADesigns Brand Skill

Use this skill when:
- Writing content for Substack, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, or email as Anita / BabicADesigns
- Generating AI image prompts for brand illustrations
- Designing any visual asset (social, covers, carousels, slides, PDFs)
- Describing BabicADesigns or Balkanish to new audiences
- Checking if a piece of writing matches the brand voice
- Building features for the Balkanish travel platform

---

## Brand in One Sentence
BabicADesigns helps emotionally intelligent solopreneurs build content systems that still sound like them — with AI, strategy, and a little Balkan spice.

## Brand Promise
> "Make AI feel human. Make business feel intentional."

## Voice
**SOULFUL. STRATEGIC. REAL.**
Trusted older sister who figured it out. Smart friend giving advice over kava. Cultural, grounded, quietly confident. Never corporate. Never guru. Never robotic.

## The Non-negotiables
1. **Voice check**: Every piece must pass all three — Soulful, Strategic, Real.
2. **Max 3 colors** per composition: Cream + Charcoal + one accent (Dusty Rose OR Sage Green).
3. **Typography**: Cormorant (hero) → Playfair (section) → EB Garamond (body). Never cross-wired.
4. **Tagline**: "The Balkanish AI Way" — always cursive script, always Dusty Rose, never restyled.
5. **Image prompts**: Always start with Master Style Instruction. Always close with "No text in image."
6. **The Drop**: Always opens "Grab your kava. This one's practical. ☕"

## Audience (One Person)
Late 30s–40s emotionally intelligent solopreneur. Tech-overwhelmed, voice-protective.
**Fear**: "AI will make me sound like everyone else."
**Desire**: "A system that works — and still sounds like me."
**Need**: "A trusted guide. Not another guru."
