# BabicADesigns Brand Glossary

**Adriatic blue** — Extended illustration palette color `#6A9AB0`. Used in background scenes, water, sky. Not part of the core 5-color brand palette.

**ajde** — Croatian/Balkan: "come on" or "let's go." Used as a warm nudge or transition in writing. Never explained to the reader.

**B mark / čipka B** — The BabicADesigns logo mark. Letter "B" constructed from Croatian lace (čipka) cross-stitch geometry. Dark Sage Green `#385048`. The visual root of the entire brand.

**Babica** — Croatian dialect: grandmother. The etymological root of "BabicADesigns." Carries connotations of handcraft, inherited wisdom, patience.

**BabicADesigns** — Full brand name. The "A" stands for Anita. Never "Babic Designs." Written as one word with internal capitals.

**Balkanish** — Hybrid adjective: "Balkan" + "-ish." Names both the travel platform and the broader aesthetic sensibility. Signals warmth, cultural specificity, without exclusion.

**The Balkanish AI Way** — The brand tagline. Always: flowing classical cursive script, Dusty Rose color, never in primary serif type.

**Baka Knows** — The WebApp product (Scene 10). AI-powered, Balkan cultural knowledge layer. Uses the 16:9 illustration format.

**Brand promise** — "Make AI feel human. Make business feel intentional." Two paired sentences — both together, neither alone.

**Carousel** — Instagram/LinkedIn content format. 5 content slides + 1 title slide. Each slide = one idea.

**Caveat Bold / Caveat Regular** — Font used in quote card text overlays. Not part of the main typography system.

**Cel-shading** — The illustration style. Flat color fills with bold linework and soft gradient shading. Editorial fashion illustration quality. NOT watercolor, painterly, or Pixar 3D.

**Charcoal (Deep Charcoal `#1C1917`)** — Typography color. All text, all formats. Not pure black — has warmth.

**Content Distribution** — Scene 09 in the canonical illustration set. Used for strategy articles. 16:9 format.

**Content pillars (Balkanish)** — Destinations, Food Finds, Culture Notes, Hidden Gems, Secret Swaps, Founder's Postcards.

**Copper džezva** — The signature prop in illustration scenes. A traditional Balkan coffee pot (džezva/džezva), copper colored. Present in nearly all café/desk scenes.

**Cormorant Garamond** — Primary display typeface. Semibold for headings, Bold for hero display. "Anything the eye should linger on."

**Creator System Series 📐** — Third content format. Alternating Thursday. 900–1,400 words. Framework deep-dive, authority building.

**čipka** — Croatian lace. The traditional craft whose geometric cross-stitch patterns form the B mark.

**Dark Sage Green `#385048`** — B mark color. In the core palette: "B Mark" role only. In illustration: background scenes, clothing, props. Extended palette code differs slightly.

**Destination Library** — Core Balkanish platform asset. Curated, AI-discoverable Balkan destinations with maps.

**The Drop ☕** — First and primary content format. Every Tuesday. 400–600 words. Workshop-style, practical value deliverable same-day. Opens with "Grab your kava. This one's practical. ☕"

**Dusty Rose `#C4A09A`** — Accent color. Tagline, one accent per composition. Never compete with Sage Green in same layout. Extended illustration code: `#C4A096`.

**EB Garamond** — Body typeface. Long-form reading, Substack, all prose blocks.

**Editorial rhythm** — The layout principle: short sentences, generous line-height, deliberate paragraph breaks, white space as structure.

**Emotionally Intelligent Solopreneur** — The target audience archetype. Late 30s–40s, tech-overwhelmed, voice-protective, fears AI homogenization.

**Extended illustration palette** — The illustration-specific color set (slightly different from core brand palette): sage green `#8B9B7A`, dusty rose `#C4A096`, warm cream `#F5EEE6`, golden blonde `#F0C878`, muted terracotta `#C4784A`, Adriatic blue `#6A9AB0`.

**Founder's Postcards** — Balkanish content pillar. Anita's personal perspective and travel writing from Croatian roots.

**The Gentle Note 🌿** — Second content format. Alternating Thursday. 600–900 words. Letter-style essay, emotional resonance.

**Golden blonde `#F0C878`** — Extended illustration palette. The protagonist's hair color in illustrations.

**Guru energy** — Anti-pattern. Performative expertise, stage-to-audience hierarchy, "unlock your potential" language. The exact opposite of this brand's voice.

**jebiga** — Croatian/Balkan: "what can you do" / rueful acceptance. Used authentically, never explained. Signals real cultural fluency.

**kava** — Balkan/Croatian: coffee. Used casually in content ("grab your kava"). The signature opening ritual of The Drop.

**Lace pattern** — The repeating B mark motif used as a background texture/watermark in physical assets (business cards, stationery).

**ma daj** — Balkan/Croatian: "oh come on." Gentle pushback, shared exasperation.

**Master Style Instruction** — The standard opening block for every AI image prompt: "clean flat cel-shading digital illustration; bold clean linework, flat color fills with soft gradient shading; NOT watercolor, NOT painterly, NOT photorealistic, NOT Pixar 3D; think high-quality editorial fashion illustration."

**Max-3 Rule** — Never more than 3 colors in a single composition. Cream + Charcoal + 1 accent.

**Muted terracotta `#C4784A`** — Extended illustration palette. Used in clothing, accessories, background details in illustrations. NOT for brand design assets (that's the "beige/terracotta" anti-pattern).

**ništa** — Balkan/Croatian: "nothing / never mind." Dismisses the obvious.

**Outfit A** — Seated/indoor: white linen blouse + sage green wide-leg trousers.

**Outfit B** — Standing/outdoor: white linen blouse + flowy sage green midi skirt + brown leather sandals.

**Outfit C** — Desk/working: white linen blouse French-tucked into high-waisted sage green straight-leg trousers.

**Pandore** — One of Anita's family origin villages in Croatia. Part of the founder story.

**Pink peonies** — Signature floral prop in illustration scenes. Dusty pink, almost always in a white ceramic vase.

**Playfair Display** — Subheading typeface. Section headers, navigation labels, secondary text hierarchy.

**polako** — Balkan/Croatian: "slowly, easy does it." Used when advising against rushing.

**Poppins Light** — Used in quote card tag labels (spaced caps). Not part of main typography system.

**Premium PDFs** — Balkanish monetization tier 1. Downloadable Balkan travel guides.

**Protagonist** — The recurring illustrated character representing Anita. Long wavy golden blonde hair, natural curvy proportions (NOT model-thin), blue-green eyes, gold hoop earrings, warm skin tone, late 30s, confident and grounded.

**Sage Green `#8A9E89`** — Secondary accent. Labels, details, nature references. Never paired as equal to Dusty Rose.

**Senj** — Croatian coastal town. Anita's ancestral home. Grounds the Balkanish platform's authenticity.

**Sister, Not Guru** — The voice archetype. Trusted older sister who figured it out, gives real talk, not performance.

**Soulful AI Strategist** — Anita's official title. Precise framing: not "AI consultant," not "content creator." Appears exactly this way on business cards.

**Substack note** — Short-form Substack platform post. Under 120 characters. One tight observation.

**Sveti Juraj** — Croatian village. Part of Anita's family roots.

**Thread** — Twitter/X multi-post format. Each post under 280 characters. Built from the hook + key points of a longer piece.

**"The Balkanish AI Way"** — See above: tagline, always cursive, always Dusty Rose.

**Typography hierarchy** — Cormorant (hero) → Playfair (section) → EB Garamond (body). Never mix more than two typefaces.

**Upper-left golden light** — "Warm golden light from upper left always." The consistent lighting direction for all illustration scenes and photography.

**Voice triad** — SOULFUL. STRATEGIC. REAL. All three must be present in every piece.

**Voice-protective** — The core fear of the target audience. They've spent years developing a distinct voice and fear AI will flatten it.

**Warm Cream `#F0E6D3`** — Base/background color. "The paper everything breathes on." Never a warm beige or terracotta substitute.

**White space** — Not absence — structure. The brand uses more margin than feels comfortable. Deliberate layout choice.
